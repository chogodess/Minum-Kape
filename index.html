Our Story
Est. 2024

Crafted with Passion,
Served with Heart

Born in the warm heart of Tarlac, Minum Kape is more than a coffee shop — it's a sanctuary for coffee lovers seeking authenticity and comfort. We believe that every cup deserves to be a moment of pure joy, brewed with care and shared with love.

From our carefully sourced local beans to our expertly trained baristas, every detail is intentional. We pour not just coffee, but stories, culture, and a little bit of home into every single order.

  • ✓
    Locally sourced, premium Philippine coffee beans
  • ✓
    Handcrafted recipes perfected by expert baristas
  • ✓
    Warm, inclusive atmosphere for everyone
  • ✓
    Community-driven, sustainable practices
